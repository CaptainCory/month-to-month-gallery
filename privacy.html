Month to Month is a Venice boardwalk art gallery and community space operated by Woodies International, LTD ("Month to Month", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, attend our events, or sign up for text messages, and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect only what you give us: your name, email address, and mobile phone number when you ask to hear about events, buy a painting, or contact us. Our website does not use advertising trackers or sell data.

How we use your information

We use your contact details to send event invitations and announcements you asked for, to respond to your messages, and to complete purchases. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

SMS / text messaging program

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except as needed to deliver the messages (for example, our SMS delivery provider) or as required by law.

  • Program: Month to Month event invitations and gallery announcements (potlucks, dinners, art nights, social events in Venice, CA).
  • How you opt in: by personally giving Cory Stout your mobile number and agreeing to receive event texts, or by texting JOIN to +1 (213) 556-2501. We do not purchase lists or text people who have not opted in.
  • Message frequency: varies, typically 1 to 4 messages per month.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one final confirmation message.
  • Help: reply HELP for help, or email me@corystout.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Sharing with service providers

We use service providers (such as our SMS delivery provider and website host) solely to operate our services. They may process your information only on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing me@corystout.com. You may stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
